# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in FacetWire a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for
different viewpoints, and responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior
includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may restrict
participation when behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful. Reports should be sent privately to the security contact listed in
`SECURITY.md`. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and respond in a
fair and proportionate manner.

This document is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
